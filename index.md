    <div class="section-head">
      <h2>About</h2>
      <p>Research driven by a simple principle: design with people, validate on real systems, and make advanced robotics usable.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="about-grid">
      <div class="card">
        <p>I am a Robotics Engineer and PhD Researcher working on human-centred robotic systems. My doctoral research at the Bristol Robotics Laboratory focuses on teleoperation interfaces for hazardous nuclear environments, including touchscreen electrovibration feedback, remote manipulation and multi-operator, multi-robot systems.</p>
        <p>Alongside teleoperation, I investigate how robots can learn motion from human demonstrations and how generated behaviour can be evaluated for human-likeness. My preferred research cycle is <strong>design → build → test with people → validate on real robotic systems</strong>.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card interests">
